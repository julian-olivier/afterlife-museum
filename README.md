## 🏛️ Executive Project Overview
This project presents an interactive **3D Virtual Museum Exhibition** utilizing a **Google Earth / Street View point-to-point flight system**. Visitors travel exclusively between significant points of interest (the Grand Rotunda, the five cultural exhibition wings, and the Synthesis Gallery) purely through the dedicated **Task Bars** (the top navigation bar and bottom quick dock) or the automated cinematic tour:

1. **🏛️ Grand Rotunda & Thesis Monument** — Curatorial introduction and comprehensive thesis statement.
2. **🪶 Wing I: Ancient Egypt** — *The Duat, the Balance of Ma'at, and the Field of Reeds* (Papyrus of Ani).
3. **🏺 Wing II: Ancient Greece** — *The Shadowy Underworld, the Homeric Psyche, and Mortal Kleos* (Attic Lekythos with Charon).
4. **⚔️ Wing III: Norse Mythology** — *The Hall of the Slain, Helheim, and the Loom of Ragnarök* (Tjängvide Image Stone).
5. **📜 Wing IV: Ancient China** — *The Dual Soul, the Yellow Springs, and the Celestial Bureaucracy* (Lady Dai Funeral Banner).
6. **🌌 Wing V: Native North America** — *The Path of Souls, Kinship, and the Celestial River* (Spiro Mounds Marine Shell Gorget).
7. **⚖️ Synthesis & Works Cited Gallery** — Comparative matrix and 14-source MLA 9th Edition bibliography.

### Controls & Navigation
* **Wing Travel via Task Bar:** Click any wing button on the **Top Task Bar** or the **Bottom Dock** to smoothly and cinematically fly directly to that wing. The active wing is highlighted with an illuminated gold pill so your current location is always obvious.
* **Pan & Look 360° (True First-Person POV):** Click and drag your mouse (or touch on mobile/tablets) to smoothly rotate your view in 360 degrees around the station at eye level without clipping outside the museum.
* **Inspect Exhibits:** Click directly on any 3D artifact, vitrine, or reading stela (or press <kbd>E</kbd>) to open the curatorial reading lightbox containing the full scholarly essay, accession plaque, and visual analysis.
* **🎬 Guided Tour Mode with Live Countdown & Auto-Pause:** Click **"Start Guided Tour"** in the top HUD to enjoy an automated cinematic flight visiting each gallery wing in sequence. Features an active live countdown timer and progress bar (`Next Wing in: Xs`). **Crucially, the countdown timer automatically pauses whenever you click to inspect an exhibit or reading plaque**, allowing you to read at your own pace without interruption, and seamlessly resumes when you close the lightbox!
